About

JSIXRON started in Binondo as a tire and hardware supplier. When clients began asking for uniforms, safety gear, and printing, we built a network of suppliers and took on the coordination ourselves — instead of turning them away.

Today we handle sourcing, quality checks, delivery, and documentation for a range of clients: government agencies, aviation, construction, and private businesses. One contact, one quote, one invoice.
