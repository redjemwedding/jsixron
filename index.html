About

Started with tires. Clients asked for more. So we built the network.

JSIXRON began as a tire and hardware supplier in Binondo. When the same buyers started asking for uniforms, safety gear, and printing, we built a network of specialist suppliers instead of turning them away.

Today one contact handles what used to take six vendors. One quote, one delivery, one invoice.
