The Company

We started with tires. Clients kept asking for more, so we built the network to say yes.

JSIXRON began as a tire and hardware supplier in Binondo. Over time, the same buyers who came for tires started asking about uniforms, safety gear, printing, and hauling. Rather than turn them away, we built relationships with specialist suppliers across Metro Manila — and took responsibility for coordinating them.

Today, one JSIXRON contact handles what used to take six vendors. You get one quote, one delivery schedule, and one invoice. Behind the scenes, our team manages the sourcing, quality checks, and logistics.
